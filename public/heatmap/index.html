Fires in Oregon by county and month (2000-2022)

Cell color: number of fires. Choose a second layer, then click a cell for the details of its fires.

Hover a cell for its information, click it for the fire details. With the keyboard: Tab to enter the heatmap, arrow keys to move, Enter to open the details.

The fires with the same path form a ribbon, colored by land; its thickness and the values of the categories follow the chosen measure ("Thickness"). By burned area, the very small fires and categories keep a minimum size to stay visible. Click a category to filter, a ribbon for its details. Keyboard: Tab, Enter, Escape.
